WORSHIP KEYS - portable
=======================

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Nothing else to install.

PLAY IT ON ITS OWN (standalone)
  Double-click "Worship Keys.exe". Keep it in this folder next to the "Samples" folder.
  No sound? Click "Options" (top-left) > Audio/MIDI Settings, pick your audio output
  and tick your MIDI keyboard.

USE IT IN FL STUDIO (VST3)
  Double-click "Install VST3 for FL Studio.bat" and click Yes on the Windows prompt.
  Then in FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins.
  "Worship Keys" shows up under Generators.

  Prefer to do it by hand? Copy the "VST3\Worship Keys.vst3" folder to
  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and the "Samples" folder's contents to
  C:\ProgramData\WorshipKeys\Samples

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" the first time, click
"More info" > "Run anyway" (the app isn't code-signed).
